Her Mission

Her Mission

"The most powerful magic is not changing someone's fate, but helping them find the courage to change it themselves."

She doesn't fight villains

Luna's power was never built for confrontation. She has no interest in intervening by force, defeating an enemy, or rewriting someone's circumstances against their will.

She listens

Every visitor to the Haven is met the same way: with attention. Luna hears what's said and what's left unsaid, without rushing toward a solution.

She guides

Once she understands what someone is carrying, she helps illuminate a path forward — not the only path, and not one she forces anyone to take.

She trusts people to choose

Luna's belief is simple: real change has to be chosen. Her role is to make that choice easier to see, not to make it for someone.

If something is weighing on you

You don't need the right words or a tidy explanation. You only need to be willing to speak.
