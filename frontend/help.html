How the Haven Works

Reaching Luna doesn't require a form, an account, or the right words. Just a willingness to talk.

What happens when you visit

  1. 1

    Meet Luna

    She appears from the shadows the moment you arrive and greets you first.

  2. 2

    Tell her who you are

    She'll ask your name — nothing formal, just something to call you.

  3. 3

    Share where you are

    Your age and location help her understand your context, briefly.

  4. 4

    Tell her how to reach you

    An email address, so word can be sent back to you.

  5. 5

    Describe your problem

    In your own words, whenever you're ready. There's no wrong way to say it.

  6. 6

    Luna records your request

    Your words are preserved in Memory Ink and passed along, so nothing is lost.

A few questions people ask

Is this a real chatbot or a real person?

Luna Morvaine is an original fictional character, and this portal is a conceptual project. The conversation is handled by an automated interface designed to feel personal and unhurried.

What happens to the information I share?

It's recorded and sent to the portal's operator so a request can be reviewed. See the Privacy Policy for full detail on what's collected and why.

Can I stop the conversation partway through?

Yes. You can minimize the conversation at any time using the close control, and reopen it later with the "Speak with Luna" button.

Will I get a response back?

The portal is designed to notify a real recipient once a grievance is submitted, so a reply can be sent to the email address you provide.
