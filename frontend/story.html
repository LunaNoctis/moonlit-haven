Her Story

The Witch Behind the Haven

"Some magic is meant to be heard."

  1. Born beneath a black moon

    Luna Morvaine was born on a night when the sky seemed to forget its stars — beneath a rare black moon that left no light to mark her arrival.

  2. A voice beneath every voice

    From childhood, she could sense the emotions hidden behind people's words: the fear beneath confidence, the loneliness behind a smile, the sentence someone couldn't quite finish.

  3. Believing it was a curse

    At first, Luna saw her gift as a burden. Afraid of hearing things she was never meant to know, she withdrew from the people around her.

  4. The Listening Oath

    Years later, she uncovered an ancient spell — The Listening Oath — which revealed the truth: her ability was never meant to control people or uncover their secrets. It was meant to help her listen.

  5. The Moonlit Haven is built

    Luna created a sanctuary between the ordinary world and the magical realm. Slowly, people began finding their way to its door — carrying problems they couldn't solve alone, and stories they were afraid to tell.

  6. The vow

    She could not change every fate or erase every hardship. But she could help people find a path through the dark — and so she made her vow.

"No voice that reaches my door shall disappear unheard."
